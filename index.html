What's going on

The grey line is what the pointer reported, with the tremor added. The dotted line is where the hand was actually going. The colored lines are the five filters. Click a row in the results table to highlight that filter.

"Error" is the average distance from the dotted line. "Lag" is how far behind it the filter runs. "Wobble" is the shake that's left once you account for the lag. With the simulation off there's no dotted line to compare against, so only the spiral score is shown.

I made this while working on TypeParkinson. It's a way to explore filters, not a medical tool.
